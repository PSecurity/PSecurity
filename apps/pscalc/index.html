PRODUCT SURFACE

Calculator, tools and local privacy

PS.Calc is built as a local-first productivity tool with offline features, local history and protected notes.

Calculator workflow

Everyday calculation, scientific tools, developer utilities, converters, formulas and session history in one offline workspace.

Developer utilities

Base, bytes, bits, time and network helpers for practical technical work.

Secure Notes

Protected local notes with PIN and optional Android biometric authentication, designed for offline use.

PRIVACY

Local-first by design

The public policy remains available as a static HTML page for Google Play review and user access.

No cloud account

The app does not require online accounts or cloud synchronization for its core features.

No external tracking

PS.Calc does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, telemetry or external tracking services.

Protected local notes

Secure Notes content remains local to the user device unless the user deletes app data or uninstalls the app.
